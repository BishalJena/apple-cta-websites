Extra-Large Widgets and Smarter Siri

The new extra-large Home Screen widget size is here. The Dashboard and List widgets now scale up to show as many as 24 habits at once, perfect if you like to dedicate a whole page to your routine.

Ask Siri about your habits

Siri now understands what's on screen in Dayline. Try asking things like "Did I meditate today?" or "Log 30 minutes of reading," and Siri will check in for you, complete with an optional note.

Spotlight search

All of your habits are now searchable from Spotlight. Tap a result to jump straight to that habit's details.

v2.0August 18, 2026

Deeper Insights

5.2 kmWeekly average

Dayline 2.0 is our biggest update yet, focused on helping you understand your progress, not just record it.

Average amounts

Habits that track quantities now show your daily and weekly averages, plus how today compares to your personal best and lowest days.

Best day of the week

A new chart highlights which weekdays you're most consistent on, so you can plan around your natural rhythm.

Summary table

Every habit now has a summary with total check-ins, longest streak, start date, and more, all in one place.

v1.4June 3, 2026

Notes and Reminders

You can now attach a note to any check-in. Use it to remember a milestone, jot down how you felt, or record what made today work.

  • Add notes when checking in, or later from the habit's calendar.
  • Set multiple reminders per habit, each with its own time and days.
  • Reminders are skipped automatically once you've already checked in.
  • Fixed an issue where widgets could take a few minutes to refresh after midnight.
v1.0March 12, 2026

Hello, Dayline

Dayline is a calm, private habit tracker for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Create a habit, check in each day, and watch your grid fill in over time.

  • Visual grids for every habit
  • Track check-ins or amounts like minutes, kilometers, or glasses of water
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • iCloud sync with no account required
  • Shortcuts and Siri support

Thank you for being here from day one.
