Privacy Policy

Dayline is designed so that your personal data stays with you. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Your habit data

Your habits, check-ins, amounts, and notes are stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled, they are synced through your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. We, the developers of Dayline, have no access to this data and cannot read, sell, or share it.

Accounts

Dayline does not require you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number through the app.

Analytics

To understand which features are used and to fix problems, Dayline may collect anonymous, aggregated usage information such as app version, OS version, device type, and which screens are opened. This information cannot be used to identify you and never includes the contents of your habits or notes.

Crash reports

If you have opted in to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Contacting us

If you email us, we'll use your email address only to reply to you. You can ask us to delete our correspondence at any time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Dayline evolves. Material changes will be noted in the release notes. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Questions? Reach us at hello@dayline.app.
